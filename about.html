About

Wynrose Equity Research is an independent research organization covering companies listed on the exchanges of countries in the GCC, principally the Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange, the Dubai Financial Market and Tadawul.

Our work is fundamental. We cover individual companies, sectors, and market structure.

We are independent. We do not manage capital, hold client assets, or accept payment from the companies we cover. All research is published free and openly.
