About

Wynrose Investment Research is an independent research firm covering companies listed on the exchanges of the Arabian Gulf, principally the Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange, the Dubai Financial Market and Tadawul.

We publish long-form research on individual companies, on sectors, and on market structure. The region's markets are growing quickly, and we are expanding our coverage across them.

We are independent. We do not manage money, hold client assets, or take payment from the companies we write about. All research is published free and openly.
