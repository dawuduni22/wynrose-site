Our Mission

The GCC is home to some of the fastest-growing capital markets in the world. Listings have multiplied over the past five years, and the region's companies are becoming more significant to global investors each year.

Wynrose exists to cover them. Our aim is to build lasting, long-form research on the region's listed companies, one name at a time, and to keep it free and open to anyone who wants to read it.

We expect these markets to matter more over time, and we intend to grow our coverage alongside them.
